= Load on a Polaris server
:description: How DCB limits the traffic it sends to a Polaris server, and what is not limited yet.

A Polaris server is shared by its library's staff, its OPAC and DCB. DCB's traffic must not take
it, or DCB's own connection to it, down.

== Streams per connection

DCB talks to Polaris over HTTP/2, where one connection carries many requests at once. Polaris
accepts at most 100 at a time on a connection (observed August 2026). Going over makes Polaris
close the connection, and every request on it fails, including patron sign-in.

DCB's Polaris clients carry at most 64 requests on one connection, whatever the shared HTTP
client allows. More requests open another connection. The shared limit for other systems is
unchanged.

== What else limits the load

* Item enrichment for one bib works on at most 4 items at a time. Each item can take several calls.
* The Polaris staff token is cached, so most calls do not sign in again.

== Not limited yet

* Live availability can still ask many Polaris bibs at once, with no limit per library.
* Bulk work (the harvest, availability sweeps) shares the clients with patron sign-in.
* Nothing moves heavy jobs out of a library's overnight maintenance window. At MOBIUS, 98.9% of
read timeouts to Polaris between August and October 2026 fell between 19:00 and 08:00 Central.
