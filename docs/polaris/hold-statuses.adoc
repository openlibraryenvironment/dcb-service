= How DCB reads a Polaris hold's status
:description: What each Polaris hold status means for an OpenRS request, and the ones DCB does not yet act on.

DCB reads the status of each hold it places in Polaris: the lender's hold for the virtual patron,
and the borrowing library's hold for the patron. Polaris's statuses (its `sysholdstatuses` list,
checked on Polaris 8.1) are read as follows.

[cols="1,1,3",options="header"]
|===
| Polaris status | DCB reads it as | Notes

| Pending, Active
| Confirmed
|

| Located
| Confirmed
| Polaris has found an item for the hold. It usually moves to Shipped within a couple of hours.

| Shipped
| Confirmed
| The item is on its way to the pickup branch.

| Held
| Ready
| The item is on the hold shelf.

| Unclaimed
| Ready
| The hold-shelf period has passed, but the item is still on the shelf and the patron can still
collect it. Polaris cancels the hold itself a few hours later.

| Cancelled
| Cancelled
| See below for a hold Polaris cancels after Unclaimed.

| Not Supplied, Expired
| Not yet acted on
| DCB records the status but does not move the request on.

| Inactive, Out
| Not yet acted on
| DCB records the status but does not move the request on.
|===

== An uncollected hold

When a patron does not collect an item, Polaris marks the borrowing library's hold Unclaimed and
then cancels it. DCB currently records that as a cancellation of the request. Recording it as
"not collected" instead is planned.
