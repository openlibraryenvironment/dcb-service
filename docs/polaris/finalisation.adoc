= Clean-up when a request finishes
:description: What the audit says when DCB removes the lender's hold at the end of a request.

When a request finishes, or is cancelled, DCB deletes the hold it placed in the lending library's
system. The request's audit records one of these:

[cols="2,3",options="header"]
|===
| Audit entry | Meaning

| Delete supplier hold request : Success
| The hold was deleted.

| Delete supplier hold request : Success (Fallback Cancel)
| Polaris would not delete the hold in its status, so DCB cancelled it. A cancelled hold stays in Polaris's history.

| Delete supplier hold : Already deleted
| The lender's system had no such hold. This is the usual case for a request that was supplied:
the lender removes a hold once its item is checked out. Nothing to do.

| Delete supplier hold : Failed
| The lender refused, or did not answer. The entry carries the lender's response; staff may need
to remove the hold by hand.

| Delete supplier hold : Skipped
| DCB could not tell whether the hold exists, or the request has no hold id.
|===

Before this release, a hold the lender no longer had was recorded as "Failed": at MOBIUS that was
761 of 908 failures between August and October 2026. A cancellation could also fail with no
detail, because its context carried no virtual patron, and then succeed seconds later on
finalisation.
